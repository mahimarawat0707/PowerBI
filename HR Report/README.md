# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide comprehensive insights into workforce performance, employee demographics, recruitment, attendance, payroll, training, and attrition metrics.

This dashboard enables HR managers and business leaders to make data-driven decisions by monitoring key HR KPIs and identifying workforce trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall workforce statistics.
- Analyze employee distribution across departments.
- Track employee attrition and turnover.
- Evaluate employee performance ratings.
- Monitor attendance and leave patterns.
- Assess recruitment effectiveness.
- Analyze payroll and compensation data.
- Track training completion rates.
