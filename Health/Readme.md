# Healthcare Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Healthcare Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution developed to monitor healthcare operations, patient appointments, department performance, revenue generation, and patient satisfaction metrics.

This dashboard provides healthcare administrators and management teams with a centralized view of key healthcare indicators, enabling faster and more informed decision-making.

---

## Dashboard Objectives

- Track patient appointments and healthcare services.
- Monitor waiting times and appointment completion rates.
- Analyze patient demographics.
- Evaluate departmental performance.
- Measure revenue generation across departments.
- Monitor patient satisfaction scores.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value Description |
|------|------------------|
| Total Patients | Total registered patients |
| Avg Waiting Time | Average patient waiting time |
| Completed Appointments | Successfully completed appointments |
| Total Revenue | Revenue generated from healthcare services |
| Avg Satisfaction Score | Average patient satisfaction rating |
| Total Appointments | Total appointments scheduled |

---

## Dashboard Features

### Patient Analysis

#### Patient by Gender
- Male vs Female patient distribution.
- Displayed using a donut chart for quick demographic insights.

#### Patient by Age Group
- Categorizes patients into age groups:
  - 0–18
  - 19–30
  - 31–45
  - 46–60
  - 60+

Helps identify the most frequently served age categories.

---

###  Waiting Time Analysis

The waiting time visual categorizes patients based on appointment waiting duration:

- 1–10 Minutes
- 11–20 Minutes
- 21–30 Minutes
- 31–40 Minutes
- 41–50 Minutes
- 51–60 Minutes

This helps identify operational bottlenecks and service efficiency.

---

### Department Performance

#### Appointments by Department

Tracks appointment volume across departments such as:

- Cardiology
- Orthopedics
- Neurology
- ICU
- Pulmonology
- Endocrinology

Useful for understanding departmental workload distribution.

---

### Revenue Analysis

#### Revenue by Department

Displays revenue generated by each department, helping management identify:

- Top-performing departments
- Revenue contribution by specialty
- Areas requiring operational improvement

---

### Appointment Details

The dashboard contains a detailed appointment table including:

- Appointment ID
- Patient ID
- Day
- Department
- Appointment Status
- Visit Type
- Consultation Fee
- Total Bill

This provides transaction-level visibility and supports detailed analysis.

---

## Interactive Filters

Users can filter dashboard data dynamically using:

- Day Filter
- Month Filter
- Clear All Option

These controls allow deeper exploration of appointment and revenue trends.
